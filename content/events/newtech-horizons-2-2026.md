## 📋 Event Overview

**NewTech Horizons – Session 2: Beyond the Data: Think. Code. Decode.**, organized by the **IEEE-WIE VSIT Student Branch**, was an interactive hands-on workshop designed to provide students with practical insights into **data exploration and its real-world applications**.

The session introduced students to concepts related to data from the basics, making them easier to understand through **practical examples, interactive activities, and engaging discussions**.

The workshop focused on developing **data-driven thinking** and helping students understand how coding can be used to **analyse, interpret, and derive meaningful insights from data**.

The session was conducted by **Mr. Kshitij Datar**, Founder & Engineer at **Aarambha**, where he works on AI-operated Go-To-Market intelligence for B2B teams. He is also a Data & AI Engineer with a focus on automating revenue workflows, with his work spanning **artificial intelligence, data, automation, and emerging technologies**.
